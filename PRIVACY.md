# Lull privacy policy

_Last updated: October 21, 2026_

Lull is a Bluetooth remote and scheduler for the BedJet 3, made by Rohan Kanjani. It is available for
iPhone and Android.

## What Lull collects

Nothing. Lull has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. It makes no network
requests.

## What stays on your phone

- **Your schedules and settings** are stored on your phone. If your phone backs up apps (iCloud backup
  or Android backup), they are included in that backup under your own account.
- **Which BedJet you use**: Lull remembers your BedJet's Bluetooth identifier and name so it can
  reconnect quickly. This stays on the phone and is not included in backups.

## Bluetooth

Lull uses Bluetooth only to talk to your BedJet and to let two phones with Lull hand the BedJet to each
other. To do that, a phone briefly broadcasts a short code derived from your BedJet's name. On Android,
Lull declares that it does not use Bluetooth to find your location, and it never asks for location
access.

## Children

Lull is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here. Questions: open an issue at
