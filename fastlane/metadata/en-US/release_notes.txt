Lull now works with the BedJet V2.

• Use the Remote to switch between Cool, Heat and Turbo, and to set fan speed, heat temperature and the shut-off timer.
• Tonight's schedule is saved to your BedJet V2 and runs on it, so your phone can sleep too.
• Schedules can use Cool, Heat, Turbo and Pause. Gentle heat and Dry are BedJet 3 only.

Also new:
• History: see what your BedJet did each night and every change you made, so you can tell in the morning what helped.
• If Lull can't find your BedJet, it now tells you what to check.
